# Privacy policy

Holdfast has no server, account, analytics or tracking. It does not collect, sell or share your data.

- **Tab addresses** are compared on your device with your lock. When a tab is blocked, its address is kept in the blocked page's own address so the tab can return later.
- **YouTube titles** are judged on your device by a model packaged with the extension.
- **Your settings and lock** are kept in the browser's extension storage. Removing the extension removes them.
- **Network:** when a YouTube filter is on, the extension requests the page of the video you open from youtube.com, with your normal YouTube cookies, to read its title and category. It contacts nothing else.

Questions: https://github.com/Assis-Mohanty/holdfast/issues
